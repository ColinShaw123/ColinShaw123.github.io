What is Volleyball?

Volleyball is a sport just like any other, and just like any other sport it is unique in so many ways. This website will go over some of the basics of volleyball such as positions, how to play, and rotations. If you find anything in this website interesting, you are encouraged to try playing volleyball for yourself.

How to Play Volleyball?

Volleyball begins with 2 teams of 6 that are separated by a net.
Typically, a coin will be flipped to determine which team served the ball first.
Then the two teams will rally, or pass the ball back and forth over the net until the ball drops on one side.
After the ball hits the floor on one side, this will count as one point for the team who did not have the ball land on their side.
This will continue until 25 points are reached, where the "set" will end.
A set is one round of 25 points, and a single volleyball game may typically go up to 3 to 5 sets.
There is an exception because the third set will only go to 15 points.
It should be mentioned that throughout the sets if there is a tie to 25 points, for example 24-24, then the set will continue until one team gains a lead of 2 points. Ex. 28-26
